# Engine portraits

Original fictional opponent portraits generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The Gentle portrait was used as a style reference for the other four. No CLI fallback was used. The artwork changes visual identity only; all difficulty settings remain Stockfish skill 0/5/10/15/20.

## gentle

Asset: `gentle.png`

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square human opponent avatar for the chess app Castlei, first of a coordinated set. Create one original fictional human face for Gentle difficulty: a friendly adult Black man with short curly hair, rounded glasses and a soft sage overshirt, relaxed encouraging smile. Style: premium hand-painted editorial portrait, clean simplified shapes, subtle gouache texture, warm natural colors, mature and welcoming, readable at 48 pixels, not photorealistic or caricature. Head and upper shoulders centered, face large, entire hair silhouette visible, generous safe margin for a circular crop. Soft even studio lighting. Plain warm parchment background, dark forest green and sage clothing with a restrained coral accent. No text, labels, frame, chess pieces, logos or watermark. Square image.
