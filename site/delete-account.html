Delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your Castlei account and cloud backups. It applies to the app “Castlei” by Ian Andwati.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Castlei and go to Settings (the gear on the home screen).
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and cloud data.
  3. Confirm. Your cloud backups and your sign-in account are deleted.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign back in and repeat the steps. That is a security check.

Or ask us to delete it

If you can no longer open the app, email andwatiian@gmail.com from the address used to sign in with the subject “Delete my Castlei account”. We delete the account and its backups, normally within 30 days, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Deleted: your sign-in account, its profile details (email, name, picture address), and every cloud backup stored for it.
  • Stays on your device: your games and training data. Delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage in Android settings. Export a backup file first if you want to keep it.
  • Anonymous diagnostics (crash reports, performance timings and usage analytics) are not linked to your account and expire automatically under Firebase's retention periods. Switch them off any time in Settings → Privacy and diagnostics.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
