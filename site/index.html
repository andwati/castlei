What you can do

Everything a serious practice session needs

Play Stockfish

Five difficulty levels from Gentle to Maximum, optional clocks, hints, takebacks and custom start positions. The engine runs on your device, so there is no waiting for a server.

Review your games

Every move is compared with the engine's best. An evaluation graph shows where a game turned, and moves are marked as inaccuracies, mistakes or blunders, plus missed or allowed forced mates.

Daily tactics

Thousands of puzzles from the open Lichess database, filtered by rating and theme, with hints and the full solution on request. They work offline, and you can add more packs.

Opening repertoires

Build your own lines, then drill them with spaced repetition at 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 day intervals. Transpositions are recognised, and opening names appear as you play.

Your library

Import and export PGN, keep studies with variations, comments and arrows, search and filter your games, and mark favourites.

Private by design

Your games live in a local database. Sign in only if you want cloud backup. Crash, performance and usage reports are anonymous and can each be switched off.
