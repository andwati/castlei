# Publishing checklist: website, Google consent screen, Play Console, billing

## 1. Website (`site/`)
Static pages generated by `python3 tool/build_site.py` (edit the constants at the top for email, name, dates, domain): landing page, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/delete-account`.

Deploy (Firebase Hosting, free plan):
```
firebase deploy --only hosting --project castlei      # live at https://castlei.web.app
```
Custom domain: Firebase console → Hosting → **Add custom domain** → `castlei.andwati.com`, then create the DNS records Firebase shows (an A/AAAA pair or CNAME, plus a TXT record for verification) at your `andwati.com` DNS provider. The TLS certificate is issued automatically. Firebase's reserved `/__/auth/...` sign-in paths keep working.

Review `site/privacy.html` and `site/terms.html` before going live (they describe the app as built today; update them when features change). Consider a short legal review.

## 4. Billing: does the app need to be published first?
**No.** The app does not have to be public or approved for production. What you need:

Google Play (one-time Castlei Plus):
1. A Play Console developer account (one-time registration fee) and a payments (merchant) profile.
2. Create the app in Play Console and upload a build with the billing permission to **internal testing** (not production). In-app products can only be created once such a build exists.
3. Create a **one-time product** (for example `castlei_plus`) under Monetize → Products → In-app products and activate it.
4. Add yourself as a **license tester** (Settings → License testing); purchases then complete without charging a card.
5. Integrate the purchase library in the app and test with the internal-testing build installed from Play (it must come from Play, not a sideloaded APK).
6. For public release: store listing, data safety, content rating and privacy URL, then a **closed test** before production. At the time of writing, personal developer accounts created after November 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days before they can apply for production access; check Google's current rules.

Apple (later): Apple Developer Program, an App Store Connect app record, a non-consumable in-app purchase, sandbox testers, and TestFlight; the first in-app purchase is reviewed together with an app version.

Until billing is connected the app shows "Unlock Plus" with a placeholder message and a hidden test unlock (tap the version in Settings seven times).

## 5. Website analytics
Google Analytics 4 through the Firebase web app "Castlei website" (measurement ID `G-CS2NHYQYX0`, set in `tool/build_site.py`). It loads only after a visitor clicks Accept in the banner, and never when the browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. Choice is stored in the browser and can be changed with "Analytics settings" in the footer; declining removes the `_ga` cookies. Google Signals and ad personalisation are off. Clicks on elements marked `data-event` are reported as events (currently `early_access_click`). The privacy policy has a matching "This website" section. View reports in Google Analytics (property linked to the Firebase project) or Firebase console → Analytics. Set the data retention period in Analytics → Admin → Data settings → Data retention (up to 14 months).
