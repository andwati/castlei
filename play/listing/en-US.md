## Full description [4000]
Castlei is a private chess companion that runs the Stockfish engine on your phone, so you can play, analyse and learn without an account or a connection.

PLAY
- Play Stockfish at five illustrated difficulty levels, from gentle to maximum strength
- Clear turn cues, clocks, drag-and-drop pieces and a board that adapts to any screen

REVIEW
- Analyse any game with an evaluation graph and engine variations
- See where the game turned and what you could have played instead
- Import PGN files and keep your games in a searchable library

LEARN
- 24 guided lessons with 72 exercises, demonstrations and hints, all offline
- Train board coordinates from either side, with 30-second challenges
- Drill tactics and build opening repertoires

TRACK
- Progress insights by opening, colour and engine difficulty
- Mistake trends and your training strengths and weaknesses

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Your games, studies and statistics stay in a local database on your device
- An account, cloud backup, crash reports and analytics are all optional and can be switched off in Settings
- Never collected: positions, moves, PGN, names or file contents; no advertising IDs

OPEN SOURCE
Castlei is free software under the GNU GPL v3.0 and includes Stockfish. Source and notices: https://castlei.andwati.com
